Soil Moisture Retrieval Using Sentinel-1 Data of Bardhaman District

SAR Backscatter Processing · Calibration · Speckle Filtering · In-situ Validation

Sentinel-1 SAR ESA SNAP ArcGIS Soil Moisture Retrieval
  • This project focuses on retrieving and mapping soil moisture across Bardhaman district using Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data. The objective is to generate reliable soil moisture estimates by applying SAR backscatter processing, calibration, speckle filtering, terrain correction, and moisture retrieval algorithms within SNAP and ArcGIS environments.
  • A secondary goal of the study is to assess the accuracy of the satellite-derived soil moisture by comparing it with available in-situ field observations, thereby evaluating the reliability and applicability of Sentinel-1 data for operational soil moisture monitoring in the region.

Symbiosis Institute of Geoinformatics

Advanced Flood Hazard Mapping and Socio-Environmental Impact Assessment of Kamrup Metropolitan Using Google Earth Engine

Multi-temporal Sentinel-1 & 2 · Population Displacement · Migration Analysis

Google Earth Engine Sentinel-1 SAR Sentinel-2 NDWI / MNDWI Night-time Lights
  • This project aims to develop an advanced flood hazard mapping framework for Kamrup Metropolitan district using Google Earth Engine (GEE). The study integrates multi-temporal Sentinel-1 SAR data to detect flood inundation and evaluates flood severity at high temporal resolution. Flood classification is refined by validating surface water signatures using Sentinel-2 reflectance indices such as NDWI, MNDWI, and SWIR-based water detection, ensuring improved accuracy of the inundation maps.
  • To understand the long-term socio-environmental implications, the project also assesses population displacement and migration patterns over the past decade. Using census layers, settlement change datasets, and multi-year land-use transitions, the study evaluates: how recurrent flooding influences population redistribution; migration hotspots and routes toward safer zones; patterns of informal settlement growth in flood-prone pockets; and changes in built-up density after repeated flood events.
  • This socio-spatial analysis is performed without field surveys, relying entirely on satellite-driven indicators such as settlement expansion, nighttime lights, land-use shift, and proximity to flood-affected zones to infer displacement and probable migration drivers.
  • The outcome demonstrates how remote sensing and GEE can be combined to generate near-real-time flood hazard maps and provide insights into human vulnerability, resilience, and climate-driven migration in rapidly urbanizing riverine environments.

Asset Mapping of Symbiosis Lavale Campus Using Drone Data

UAV Photogrammetry · Orthomosaic & DSM Generation · Infrastructure Inventory

UAV / Drone Drone2Map Photogrammetry ArcGIS DSM / 3D
  • This project involves creating a comprehensive asset map of the Symbiosis Lavale campus using high-resolution drone imagery. Decision Tree · Random Forest · SVM · Accuracy Benchmarking

PROJECT 05

Establishing the Relationship Between Forest Cover Change and Climatic Variability in the Western Ghats

Multi-temporal Imagery · Wildfire Dynamics · Climate–Fire Analysis (2001–2015)

Landsat / Sentinel MODIS Fire Land Surface Temperature Rainfall Analysis Time Series
  • This project investigates how forest cover change in the western part of the Western Ghats is influenced by climatic variability. The study analyzes multi-temporal satellite imagery to assess forest loss, degradation, and land cover transitions, and correlates these changes with climatic factors such as rainfall patterns and land surface temperature (LST).
  • The research also incorporates wildfire dynamics as an important disturbance agent in the Western Ghats. Using long-term fire activity records (2001–2015), the project examines temporal and spatial patterns of wildland fires and evaluates how inter-annual climate variability contributes to fluctuations in burned area.
  • At both regional and landscape scales, the analysis reveals notable variations in fire frequency — showing that years like 2000, 2013, and 2022 experienced nearly five times more fire activity compared to low-fire years. Climate–fire relationships ranged from weak to strong depending on the landscape, suggesting that climatic episodes (particularly extreme LST and rainfall anomalies) may increasingly influence wildfire behavior and subsequent forest cover change.
  • The outcomes of this project provide insights into climate-driven ecological changes in one of India's most sensitive biodiversity hotspots, contributing to improved conservation planning and environmental management.

Urban Encroachment Analysis and Expansion in the Eastern Kolkata Wetlands

Ramsar Site · MOLUSCE Change Modeling · Future Scenario Simulation

MOLUSCE Landsat / Sentinel QGIS / ArcGIS Future Scenarios Wetland Conservation
  • This project investigates the patterns, drivers, and impacts of urban encroachment on the Eastern Kolkata Wetlands (EKW). Using multi-temporal satellite imagery and geospatial analysis tools, the study quantifies historical changes in land use and identifies the rate of wetland shrinkage over time. MOLUSCE is employed to model land cover transitions and to predict future scenarios of wetland loss and urban expansion under human and climatic influences.
  • The research assesses how anthropogenic pressures and environmental factors contribute to the degradation of this Ramsar site, offering insights into sustainability challenges and supporting future conservation and policy planning for the Eastern Kolkata Wetlands.

Visualization of Land Subsidence Due to Coal Mining Using Sentinel-1 Data in Paschim Bardhaman District

SAR Interferometry · Deformation Analysis · Mining-induced Geohazard Mapping

Sentinel-1 SAR ESA SNAP SAR Interferometry ArcGIS Geohazard Mapping
  • This project focuses on detecting and visualizing land subsidence in the coal mining regions of Paschim Bardhaman district using Sentinel-1 SAR data. The study utilizes SAR interferometry and deformation analysis techniques to identify ground displacement caused by extensive underground mining activities.
  • By processing Sentinel-1 imagery — including calibration, filtering, and interferogram generation — the project produces detailed subsidence maps, highlighting zones of significant vertical ground movement. ArcGIS is used to visualize and interpret the spatial patterns, enabling better understanding of mining-induced geohazards and supporting risk assessment and regional planning.
